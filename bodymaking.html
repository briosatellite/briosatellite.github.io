I am making a body. There are lots of considerations for making a body. What is the body made of? I am making a body in my own image. My body is made of wire tension circuitry metal skin. The body I want has unconventional intimates. The body I want cannot be made with surgery. The body I want can be made with leather. The body I want requires tools.

I use three blades to cut leather. I mark diodes with hole punches, and passages, too. I bevel, I burnish. The body I want feels nice.

My last creation had different charms. I made a body of wire. It cut in and constricted, it scuffed, it was tedious. I like that it’s showy, I like that the pain is visible, provocative. The good news is it doesn’t fit me anymore.

This device mediates and facilitates torture. It takes reference from a kidney belt on display at the Old Melbourne Gaol. Its general aim is to protect the kidneys from being ruptured during a flogging. This is an awful origin for a piece, but it’s also sexy. I use a strap cutter, black gum on the edges, a stitching iron, a skiving blade, waxed thread, a stitching pony, an awl, saddle needles, brass buckles. The shoulder that braces hurts more than the one that one that drives. The hands ache equally. It’s a lot of time and effort to make corporal punishment safer to endure. The body I want is not being tortured, but it does have protected kidneys.

The body I want can draw, again. The body I want never stopped drawing. The body I want can use their hands all day. The body I want has never doubted their physical capacity to work in any field they love. I consider abandoning fashion to focus on being a gym rat. The body I want has never stocked shelves. The body I want never sacrificed their health for a supermarket. The body I want can walk in boots, can walk long distances, and can walk without error. The body I want can sit, and stand, and catch the train, and rest, equally. The body I want is not a project.
